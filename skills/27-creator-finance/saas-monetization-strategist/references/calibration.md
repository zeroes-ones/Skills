# Calibration — How to Know Your Level in Saas Monetization Strategist

<!-- STANDARD: 3min -- honest self-assessment rubric -->

| You Know You're Stuck at L1 When... | You Know You've Reached L2 When... | You Know You're L3 When... |
|---|---|---|
| You can follow a tutorial but can't adapt to new requirements without guidance | You receive a feature request and independently design, build, test, and ship it | You define technical direction for a product area and designs hold up under 10x growth |
| You copy-paste patterns without understanding why one approach works and another fails | You can explain trade-offs between approaches and choose deliberately based on context | Teams seek your architecture advice and your recommendations consistently prove correct |
| You get stuck on unfamiliar errors and need someone else to debug them | You trace errors from user-facing symptom through the stack to root cause | You identify whether the fix needs code, config, infrastructure, or process changes |
| You implement exactly what's asked without questioning the real problem | You push back on bad requirements and propose better alternatives | Product managers bring you in during ideation, not after the PRD is written |

**The Litmus Test:** Can you take a one-sentence idea and independently deliver a production-quality implementation? If you need detailed specs to get started, you're not L3 yet.
