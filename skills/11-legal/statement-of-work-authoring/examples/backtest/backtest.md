## Scenario analysis

Best case: scope frozen at signature, acceptance criteria met in the first review window, and
milestone payments clear on schedule. Worst case: scope crosses the 15–20% trigger with no
change request, acceptance stalls, and disputes cost **$3,000–$15,000**. Lesson learned: the
three clauses that matter most are the one-sentence scope boundary, testable acceptance, and a
written 15–20% change-control trigger — every dispute in the log traces to a missing one.

**Complete when:** the SOW log shows ≥3 engagements and the checklist defaults trace to the
outcomes above, not to a template you copied.
