# Business Associate Agreement (BAA) Registry

## When a BAA is Required
Any vendor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on your behalf:
- Cloud hosting (AWS, Azure, GCP) — HIPAA-eligible services only
- EHR/EMR vendors
- Analytics platforms receiving PHI
- SMS/notification services sending appointment reminders
- Backup/disaster recovery services
- Shredding companies handling paper records

## BAA Checklist
- [ ] Names both parties (Covered Entity + Business Associate)
- [ ] Specifies permitted uses of PHI
- [ ] Requires breach notification within 60 days
- [ ] Requires subcontractor BAAs with same terms
- [ ] Specifies data return/destruction at contract end
- [ ] HIPAA Security Rule compliance commitment
- [ ] HIPAA Privacy Rule compliance commitment

## Common BAA Gaps ($50K-$1.5M fines)
- **No BAA with analytics vendor** — Google Analytics on patient portal without BAA
- **Subcontractor chain break**: BA subcontracts to cloud provider, no BAA cascade
- **Verbal only**: "Yeah, we're HIPAA compliant" — no signed agreement
