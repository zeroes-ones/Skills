# Tax Receipt Compliance
> Reference: IRS Pub 1771, CRA, Charity Commission requirements

## IRS Requirements (US 501(c)(3))

### Required Fields
1. Organization name
2. EIN (Employer Identification Number)
3. Donor name (as it appears on tax return)
4. Date of contribution
5. Amount of cash contribution (or description of non-cash)
6. Statement: "No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution" (OR description + FMV of goods/services received)

### Thresholds
- Donations <$250: Bank record sufficient, no written receipt required
- Donations ≥$250: Written acknowledgment required before filing
- Non-cash >$500: Additional form 8283 required
- Non-cash >$5,000: Qualified appraisal required

## CRA Requirements (Canada)
- Official donation receipt must include: charity name, BN/Registration number, donor name, date, amount, eligible amount, signature
- Receipt must be issued by end of February following tax year

## Charity Commission (UK)
- Gift Aid declarations required for tax-effective giving
- Charity must retain Gift Aid declarations for 6 years
- Receipt not legally required but recommended best practice

## Recurring Donation Receipts
- Monthly receipts: issued per charge (compliance for donors who need per-payment records)
- Annual summary: optional, sent January of following year
- Summary must list each individual donation date and amount
