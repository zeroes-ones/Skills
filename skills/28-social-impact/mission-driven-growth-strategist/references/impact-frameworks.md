# Impact Measurement Frameworks

## Framework Comparison

| Framework | Owner | Focus | Metrics | Best For |
|-----------|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| **IRIS+** | GIIN (Global Impact Investing Network) | Investor-facing impact measurement | 700+ standardized metrics across sectors | Impact investors, social enterprises raising capital |
| **SDG Impact Standards** | UNDP | SDG alignment | SDG targets + indicators | UN alignment, public sector, development |
| **B Impact Assessment** | B Lab | Holistic stakeholder impact | 200+ questions, 5 categories | Certification, holistic assessment |
| **SROI** | Social Value International | Monetized social value | Ratio: social value / investment | Cost-benefit analysis, funder reporting |
| **GRI** | Global Reporting Initiative | Sustainability reporting | Universal + topic-specific standards | Public reporting, ESG |
| **SASB** | Sustainability Accounting Standards Board | Industry-specific materiality | 77 industry standards | Investor-grade ESG data |

## IRIS+ Core Metrics

IRIS+ (Impact Reporting and Investment Standards) is the most widely used framework in impact investing.

### Core Metrics for Any Social Enterprise

| IRIS+ Code | Metric | Definition |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| **PI4060** | Client Individuals: Total | Number of unique individuals who were clients during the reporting period |
| **PI3687** | Jobs Created | Number of full-time equivalent jobs created at the organization |
| **OI5411** | CO2 Emissions Reduced | Amount of CO2 emissions reduced by the organization's products/services |
| **PI2828** | Client Individuals: Poor | Number of unique poor clients (using national poverty line) |
| **PI8330** | Client Individuals: Female | Number of unique female clients |
| **PD2984** | Average Client Household Income | Average household income of clients (baseline vs endline) |

### Sector-Specific Metrics Examples

**Agriculture:**
- PI3796: Area of sustainably managed land (hectares)
- PI4849: Average agricultural yield increase (%)
- OI1128: Average client income from agricultural activities

**Education:**
- PI3638: Student attendance rate (%)
- PI4177: Student graduation rate (%)
- PI4247: Student test score improvement (%)

**Health:**
- PI4241: Patients served (number)
- PI2726: Health outcome improvement (%)
- PI4706: Patient satisfaction rate (%)

**Financial Inclusion:**
- PI6330: Average loan size disbursed
- PI8210: Average client savings balance
- PI4955: Client retention rate (%)

## B Impact Assessment Question Categories

The BIA has 5 impact areas with ~200 questions (weighted by company size, sector, geography).

| Impact Area | Weight | Sample Questions |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Governance** | ~20% | Mission statement in governing documents? Stakeholder consideration in decision-making? Transparency reporting? |
| **Workers** | ~25% | Living wage paid? Benefits and training? Ownership and equity sharing? Workplace safety and flexibility? |
| **Community** | ~25% | Local hiring and suppliers? Diversity and inclusion? Civic engagement? Charitable giving? |
| **Environment** | ~20% | Environmental management system? Carbon footprint measured? Renewable energy use? Supply chain environmental screening? |
| **Customers** | ~10% | Product/service addresses social problem? Customer data privacy? Ethical marketing? Feedback mechanisms? |

**Target score:** 80+ points for certification. Median score: ~51 points across all companies.

### B Impact Assessment Scoring Strategy

1. Start 12-18 months before target certification
2. Complete baseline assessment — identify lowest-scoring areas
3. Improve operations BEFORE finalizing assessment (assessment reflects operations, not intentions)
4. Focus on areas with highest weight and lowest current score
5. Document evidence for every claimed practice (B Lab verifies)

## SROI (Social Return on Investment) Methodology

### Formula

**SROI Ratio = (Social Value Created) / (Total Investment)**

### Calculation Steps

1. **Establish scope and identify stakeholders** — Who changes? How?
2. **Map outcomes** — What changes for each stakeholder?
3. **Evidence outcomes and give them value** — Use financial proxies
4. **Establish impact** — Account for deadweight, attribution, displacement, drop-off
5. **Calculate SROI ratio** — Total value / total investment
6. **Report, use, embed** — Share findings, inform decisions

### Financial Proxies (Examples)

| Outcome | Financial Proxy | Value Estimate |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Improved health | Reduced healthcare costs | $X/year (local clinic visit cost × frequency) |
| Increased income | Additional income | $X/year (difference in income pre/post) |
| Reduced recidivism | Reduced prison costs | $X/year (daily incarceration cost × days) |
| Educational attainment | Increased lifetime earnings | $X (NPV of earnings difference) |
| Reduced homelessness | Reduced shelter costs | $X/year (shelter bed cost per night × 365) |

### SROI Quality Principles

1. Involve stakeholders
2. Understand what changes
3. Value the things that matter (use financial proxies)
4. Only include what is material
5. Do not over-claim (account for attribution, deadweight)
6. Be transparent
7. Verify the result (independent assurance recommended)

**Typical SROI ratios:**
- Employment programs: $2-$6 per $1 invested
- Health interventions: $3-$11 per $1 invested
- Education programs: $4-$16 per $1 invested
- Criminal justice: $2-$8 per $1 invested

**Warning:** SROI ratios are NOT comparable across organizations. Each uses different proxy values, assumptions, and scope. Compare SROI within the same organization over time, not between organizations.
