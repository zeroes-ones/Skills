# Stakeholder Governance Patterns

## Multi-Stakeholder Board Design

### Board Composition Template

| Seat Type | % of Board | Selection Method | Term | Role |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|------|------|
| **Independent Directors** | 40-50% | Nominating committee, board vote | 3 years | Fiduciary oversight, strategy, CEO evaluation |
| **Founder/Executive** | 10-20% | Founder seat, CEO seat | Ongoing | Institutional knowledge, mission vision |
| **Investor Representatives** | 10-20% | Investor agreement | Per agreement | Capital oversight, financial sustainability |
| **Worker Representatives** | 10-15% | Worker election | 2-3 years | Operational reality, staff perspective |
| **Beneficiary Representatives** | 5-15% | Beneficiary election or community nomination | 2-3 years | Lived experience, impact validation |
| **Community/Subject Matter Experts** | 5-10% | Board appointment | 3 years | Sector expertise, community relationships |

### Beneficiary Board Representation Design

**Prerequisites for effective beneficiary representation:**
1. Board orientation in beneficiary's primary language
2. Financial literacy training before first board meeting
3. Pre-meeting briefings (1-2 weeks before each meeting)
4. Transportation and childcare stipends
5. Meeting scheduling that accommodates beneficiary work schedules
6. Translation services during meetings (simultaneous if possible)

**Decision rights for beneficiary seats:**
- Full voting rights (not advisory — voting)
- Equal access to board materials (same documents, same timing)
- Seat on at least one committee (audit, program, or governance)
- Right to add items to board agenda

## Golden Share Mechanics

A **golden share** is a special share held by a trust, foundation, or designated guardian with veto power over mission-critical decisions.

### Protected Decisions (Golden Share Veto)

| Decision | Veto Threshold | Rationale |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Mission change | Golden share veto | Prevents mission drift |
| Asset sale (>X% of assets) | Golden share veto | Protects asset lock |
| Entity conversion (e.g., benefit corp → standard corp) | Golden share veto | Prevents legal structure erosion |
| Merger or acquisition | Golden share veto or supermajority | Ensures mission survives transaction |
| Amendment of golden share provisions | Golden share + 75% shareholder vote | Prevents dilution of golden share power |
| Dissolution | Golden share + board + shareholder vote | Ensures assets go to mission-aligned successor |

**Golden Share Holder:** [Name of trust, foundation, or designated individual]
**Selection:** [How is the golden shareholder selected/appointed? Succession plan?]
**Decisions Covered:** [List of decisions requiring golden share approval]
**Removal/Replacement:** [Can the golden shareholder be removed? By whom? Under what conditions?]
**Duration:** [Perpetual or time-limited? Survives acquisition?]

### Golden Share Implementation Options

**Option 1: Foundation-owned Golden Share**
- Create a mission-aligned foundation (501(c)(3) or equivalent)
- Foundation holds one golden share with defined veto rights
- Foundation board is independent of company board
- Foundation's sole purpose: protect the mission

**Option 2: Trust Structure**
- Create a purpose trust (like Patagonia Purpose Trust)
- Trust holds voting stock (2% of total shares, 100% of voting rights)
- Trust beneficiaries are the mission (not individuals)
- Trust protectors oversee trustee decisions

**Option 3: Designated Guardian**
- Individual (or small committee) holds golden share
- Typically founder, respected elder, or community leader
- Succession plan required: who holds golden share after founder
- Risk: single point of failure — mitigate with clear succession

## Community Advisory Council (CAC)

### CAC Design Template

**Purpose:** Provide formal community/beneficiary input on organizational decisions.

**Composition:**
- 7-15 members representing beneficiary demographics
- At least 50% current/past beneficiaries
- Geographic, gender, age diversity reflecting served population

**Authority:**
- Advisory (not governance — does not replace board)
- Formal response requirement: board must respond in writing to CAC recommendations within 60 days
- CAC may escalate to golden shareholder if board repeatedly ignores recommendations

**Meeting Cadence:**
- Quarterly meetings (minimum)
- At least one joint meeting with board annually
- Beneficiary feedback sessions between CAC meetings for broader input

**Budget:**
- Stipends for CAC members (competitive with local wages)
- Meeting costs: venue, food, transportation, childcare, translation
- Annual budget: $15K-$50K depending on location and size

### CAC Engagement Topics

| Topic | CAC Role | Board Obligation |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Program design changes | Review and recommend | Respond in writing within 60 days |
| New geography/program expansion | Input on community need and readiness | Include CAC input in board decision memo |
| Fee/pricing changes for beneficiaries | Recommend affordability thresholds | Respond + justify any deviation |
| Impact metric selection | Define what "success" means from beneficiary perspective | Incorporate at least 50% of CAC-preferred indicators |
| Annual impact report | Review and validate findings | Incorporate CAC validation statement in report |

## Decision Rights Matrix

| Decision | Board Vote | Golden Share | Stakeholder Consultation | CEO Authority |
|----------|-----------|-------------|------------------------|---------------|
| Mission change | 75% supermajority | Veto | CAC + staff | None |
| Annual budget | Simple majority | None | None | Proposes |
| Executive hiring/firing | Simple majority | None | Staff (for hiring) | Proposes candidates |
| New program launch | Simple majority | None | CAC input | Proposes |
| Program closure | Simple majority | None | CAC + affected staff | Proposes |
| Asset sale >20% | 75% supermajority | Veto | None | None |
| Merger/acquisition | 75% supermajority | Veto | CAC + staff | None |
| Executive compensation | Simple majority | None | None | Benchmark recommendation |
| Strategic partnerships | Simple majority | None | None | Negotiates |
| Debt incurrence >5% of assets | Simple majority | None | None | Proposes |

## Governance Maturity Model

| Level | Board Composition | Stakeholder Voice | Accountability |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **L1: Basic** | Founder + investors only | No formal mechanism | Mission statement only |
| **L2: Developing** | 1-2 independent directors | Annual beneficiary survey | Benefit report (if benefit corp) |
| **L3: Established** | 40% independent, diverse | Community advisory council with response requirement | B Corp certification, published impact report |
| **L4: Advanced** | Multi-stakeholder (workers + beneficiaries) | Beneficiary voting board seat + CAC | Golden share, steward-ownership overlay |
| **L5: Transformative** | Community-majority board | Beneficiary governance (cooperative model) | Asset lock, perpetual mission protection |
