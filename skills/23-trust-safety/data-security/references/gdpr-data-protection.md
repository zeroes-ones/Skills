# GDPR — Data Protection Articles

## Overview
General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679) establishes data protection principles and obligations for processing EU personal data.

## Key Articles for Data Security

### Art. 5 — Principles of Data Processing
- Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
- Purpose limitation
- **Data minimization** (adequate, relevant, limited)
- **Storage limitation** (no longer than necessary)
- Integrity and confidentiality (security)

### Art. 25 — Data Protection by Design and by Default
- Technical measures integrated into processing
- Data minimization by default
- Only necessary personal data processed

### Art. 32 — Security of Processing
- Appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Pseudonymization and encryption
- Resilience, availability, restoration
- Regular testing and evaluation

### Art. 44-49 — Cross-Border Data Transfers
- Adequacy decisions (Art. 45)
- Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46)
- Binding Corporate Rules (Art. 47)
- Derogations for specific situations (Art. 49)

## Enforcement
- Fines up to EUR 20M or 4% of global annual turnover
- Both controllers and processors liable
