# EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF)

## Overview
European Commission adequacy decision (July 10, 2023) establishing the EU-US Data Privacy Framework as a valid transfer mechanism under GDPR Article 45.

## Certification Requirements
- US organizations must self-certify annually to the US Department of Commerce
- Public commitment to DPF Principles
- Enforceable under US law (FTC/DoT enforcement)

## DPF Principles
1. Notice: Inform individuals about data processing
2. Choice: Opt-out of disclosures to third parties
3. Accountability for Onward Transfer: Contracts with processors
4. Security: Reasonable and appropriate measures
5. Data Integrity and Purpose Limitation
6. Access: Individual access to their data
7. Recourse, Enforcement, and Liability

## Redress Mechanisms
- Independent dispute resolution (free to EU individuals)
- Data Protection Review Court (DPRC)
- Binding arbitration as final recourse
- EU individuals can lodge complaints with their DPA

## Limitations
- Only covers US organizations on the DPF List
- Does not apply to all US data importers
- Must be reviewed periodically by EU Commission
- Potential legal challenge (Schrems III risk)
