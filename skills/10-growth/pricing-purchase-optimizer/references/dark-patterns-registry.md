# Dark Patterns Registry — Do NOT Implement

## Category 1: Forced Continuity

### Pattern: Free trial requires credit card, auto-converts to paid without adequate reminder
**FTC Status:** Enforcement action since 2021. Requires clear disclosure AND simple cancellation.
**Legal Risk:** FTC fines $43,792 per violation. Class action lawsuits.
**Compliant Alternative:** Send reminder email 3-7 days before trial converts. One-click cancellation.

### Pattern: "Cancel anytime" but cancellation requires phone call during business hours
**FTC Status:** "Click to Cancel" rule (proposed 2023): cancellation must be as easy as signup.
**Compliant Alternative:** Cancel button in account settings. Confirmation, not retention dark pattern.

## Category 2: Hidden Costs

### Pattern: Tax/VAT added at final step — price increases 20% at payment
**FTC Status:** Unfair/deceptive practice. Must disclose total cost.
**Compliant Alternative:** Show tax-inclusive price before payment. Geo-detect and display applicable rate.

### Pattern: "Free shipping" but handling fee added at checkout
**FTC Status:** Deceptive pricing. Advertised price must include mandatory fees.
**Compliant Alternative:** Show total cost including all mandatory fees at product/plan selection.

## Category 3: Bait and Switch

### Pattern: Advertising $9/mo pricing but that tier has no usable features
**FTC Status:** Deceptive advertising. Advertised product must be functional.
**Compliant Alternative:** All advertised tiers must be genuinely usable. Low tier can have limits; can't be non-functional.

### Pattern: "Starting at $X" but minimum usable plan is $3X
**FTC Status:** Deceptive if starting tier is non-functional for advertised use case.
**Compliant Alternative:** Advertise the price of the plan that delivers the advertised value.

## Category 4: Confirmshaming

### Pattern: "No thanks, I don't want to save money" as decline option
**FTC Status:** Not yet specifically regulated but increasingly cited in consumer complaints.
**Compliant Alternative:** Neutral decline option: "Maybe later" or "Continue with monthly."

## Category 5: Sneak into Basket

### Pattern: Pre-checked add-on (insurance, priority support, extended warranty)
**FTC Status:** Unfair practice. Must be opt-in, not opt-out.
**Compliant Alternative:** Unchecked by default. Clear opt-in: "Add priority support (+$19/mo)".

## Category 6: Hidden Subscription

### Pattern: One-time purchase page that actually enrolls in subscription (fine print)
**FTC Status:** ROSCA violation. Material terms must be clear and conspicuous.
**Compliant Alternative:** Clear labeling: "Monthly subscription — $29/month. Cancel anytime."

## Category 7: Roach Motel

### Pattern: Easy to sign up, nearly impossible to cancel
**FTC Status:** "Click to Cancel" rule. Cancellation must mirror enrollment ease.
**Compliant Alternative:** Self-serve cancellation. One-click from account. Confirmation email immediately.

## EU Consumer Protection (Additional)

### Digital Content Directive (2022)
- 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content
- Must provide explicit pre-contractual information
- Free trial auto-conversion requires explicit consent, not passive

### Omnibus Directive (2022)
- Price reduction claims must reference lowest price in last 30 days
- Fake reviews prohibited (verified purchase only)
- Search result ranking manipulation must be disclosed
