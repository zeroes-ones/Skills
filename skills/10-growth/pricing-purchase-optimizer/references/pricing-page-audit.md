## Quick Wins Checklist

These are high-impact, low-effort fixes that typically improve conversion 5-15%:
- [ ] Add "Most Popular" badge to target tier
- [ ] Default toggle to annual view
- [ ] Add customer logos above pricing
- [ ] Change CTA from "Submit" to "Start Free Trial"
- [ ] Show total cost before payment details
- [ ] Stack tiers vertically on mobile
