## Key Reports
- **WASDE (World Agricultural Supply and Demand Estimates)**: Monthly, ~12th of month, 12:00 ET — global supply/demand balance sheets
- **Crop Progress**: Weekly (Monday 16:00 ET) — planting/harvesting progress, crop conditions
- **Prospective Plantings**: March 31 — acreage intentions
- **Acreage Report**: June 30 — actual planted acreage
- **Quarterly Grain Stocks**: March, June, September, December 31

## Seasonality Drivers
- **Planting**: April-May (US corn/soybeans), weather premium
- **Growing season**: June-August — weather risk peak (drought, flooding)
- **Harvest**: September-November — harvest pressure, basis weakens
- **South American crop**: Planting October-November, harvest February-March
