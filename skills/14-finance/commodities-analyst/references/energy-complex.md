## Key EIA Reports
- **Weekly Petroleum Status Report (WPSR)**: Wednesdays 10:30 ET — crude inventories, production, refinery utilization
- **Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO)**: Monthly — price forecasts, supply/demand balance
- **Drilling Productivity Report (DPR)**: Monthly — per-well productivity by basin

## Natural Gas Fundamentals
- **Storage**: EIA Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report (Thursdays 10:30 ET)
- **Weather**: HDD (Heating Degree Days) and CDD (Cooling Degree Days) forecasts
- **Production**: Dry gas production by basin (Haynesville, Appalachia, Permian)
- **LNG exports**: Feedgas flows to liquefaction terminals (Cheniere, Freeport)

## Crack Spreads
- **3-2-1 Crack**: 3 crude → 2 gasoline + 1 distillate (refining margin proxy)
- **Gasoline Crack**: RBOB - WTI (seasonal: peaks in spring)
- **Distillate Crack**: Heating Oil - WTI (seasonal: peaks in fall/winter)
