# Commodity Cycles Reference

## The Super Cycle Framework
Commodity super cycles last 15-25 years, driven by structural demand shifts.

| Super Cycle | Period | Driver |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Industrialization (US) | 1900-1919 | US industrial revolution |
| Post-War Reconstruction | 1946-1970 | Europe/Japan rebuilding |
| China Industrialization | 2000-2014 | China urbanization, infrastructure |
| Energy Transition? | 2020-? | Electrification, decarbonization |

## Within-Cycle Dynamics
```
High Prices → Investment/Capex → New Supply (lagged 3-7 years) → Oversupply → Low Prices → Capex Cuts → Supply Decline → High Prices
```

## Capital Expenditure (Capex) Cycle
- **Upstream oil**: Years of underinvestment (2015-2023) → supply constraints
- **Copper**: Major new mines take 5-10 years from discovery to production
- **Lithium/Rare Earths**: Capex boom in 2021-2023 → oversupply in 2024-2025

## China Cycle Dominance
- China consumes: 55% of copper, 55% of iron ore, 15% of oil, 60% of soybeans
- China credit impulse → commodity demand (3-6 month lead)
- China PMI vs copper price: 0.6 correlation over 20 years

## Dollar Cycle
- Commodities priced in USD → DXY inverse relationship
- DXY up 10% → commodity index down ~5-7% (historically)
- Strong dollar = tighter EM financial conditions = lower commodity demand
