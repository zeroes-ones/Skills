## Key LME Mechanics
- **3-month forward**: Benchmark contract, rolls daily
- **Prompt dates**: Daily out to 3 months, weekly to 6 months, monthly to 123 months
- **Carries**: Contango or backwardation between prompt dates
- **Warrants**: Warehouse receipts representing physical metal

## Inventory Analysis
- **LME warehouse reports**: Daily — on-warrant vs canceled warrants
- **SHFE inventory**: Weekly (Friday) — China's primary metals exchange
- **COMEX inventory**: Daily — gold and silver warehouse stocks

## Key Ratios
- **Gold/Silver Ratio**: Measure of relative value; long-term median ~60
- **Copper/Gold Ratio**: Industrial demand vs safe haven proxy (macro indicator)
- **Gold/Oil Ratio**: Inflation-adjusted gold vs energy cost
