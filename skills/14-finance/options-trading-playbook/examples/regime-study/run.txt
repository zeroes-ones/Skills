2. PRE-REGISTERED STRATEGIES - headline trade and equity metrics
====================================================================================================
p = win rate | b = avg-win/avg-loss
E = p*b-(1-p)  -- the pre-registered formula.  It is expectancy in units
                 of the AVERAGE LOSS, not in R: E = E_R / |avg_loss|.
                 The two coincide only when every loss is exactly -1R.
E_R = mean R   -- true expectancy per trade in R.  This is the honest one.
p* = breakeven win rate 1/(1+b) | b* = required payoff (1-p)/p (both exact)
sd = sd of R | n_req = 4*(sd/|E|)^2 trades needed for ~2 sigma
L = expected longest losing streak ln(N)/ln(1/(1-p)); Lobs = observed
CAGR/DD/Sharpe from the 1%-risk daily equity curve; Sharpe = daily
mean/sd x sqrt(252), Rf = 0, full sample as lookback.
B&H = buy and hold over the same window (price-only / total return).
