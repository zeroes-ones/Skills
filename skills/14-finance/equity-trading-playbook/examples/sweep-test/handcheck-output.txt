-- CHECK 3: the pre-registered default over 2015, computed by hand ----------------------------
    the book is 4 equal sleeves, 25.0000% each, N=200, monthly rebalance.
    a sleeve holds the bar-to-bar price move on every session it is long and
    holds cash on every session it is flat; the book is the sum of the sleeves.
    the year runs 2015-01-02 .. 2015-12-31 (252 sessions).
    convention: a calendar-year return is the product of the returns EARNED on
    the year's sessions, i.e. measured from the prior session's close -- the same
    convention study.py uses, so the base is the session before 2015-01-02.
    equity on the session before the year = 180.9889515776  (2014-12-31)
    equity on the last  2015 session       = 175.0496629630  (2015-12-31)
    year return by hand = (175.0496629630 / 180.9889515776) - 1
                        = -0.0328157524 = -3.281575%
