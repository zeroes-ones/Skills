## 2. The cash series, and why the lambda=0 column is the cash rate

`r(lambda) = lambda * r_risky + (1 - lambda) * r_cash`.  At `lambda = 0`
this is `r_cash` term by term, so the `lambda = 0` row of every frontier
table below is the cash proxy and nothing else.  The gap between the
independently computed cash CAGR and the `lambda = 0` CAGR is printed as
an identity check, not asserted:

- R4 cash CAGR over its window: **1.7809%**
- R2L cash CAGR over its window: **1.9777%**

The cash proxy is `TVC:US03M`, the 3-month US Treasury bill YIELD INDEX.
A yield index is not a fund, and it is also not positive every day.
