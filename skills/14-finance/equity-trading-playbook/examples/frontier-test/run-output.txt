(8) THE FIRST 5% OF RISK IS THE EXPENSIVE PART
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The frontier is not linear in lambda, and it is not the flat days that
  make it so.  Almost the whole fall in positivity happens between
  lambda=0 and lambda=0.05 -- between holding cash and holding 95% cash.
  At lambda=0.05 the risky term contributes 0.05 of the book's daily swing
  while cash contributes 0.95 of roughly one daily accrual, so the SIGN of
  the day's return is decided by the risky sliver.  Measured:

  book  book daily sd  cash accrual/day  risky-sd / cash at lam=0.05  D>0 @lam0  D>0 @lam0.05  D>0 @lam1
  ----  -------------  ----------------  ---------------------------  ---------  ------------  ---------
  R4    0.51%          0.0070%           3.6                          99.6%      66.2%         52.1%
  R9    0.44%          0.0064%           3.5                          99.6%      66.0%         53.2%
  R10   0.40%          0.0057%           3.5                          99.6%      66.0%         53.1%
  R2L   0.83%          0.0078%           5.3                          99.7%      70.9%         43.0%
  R1L   0.75%          0.0099%           3.8                          99.7%      73.8%         40.6%

  A SEPARATE, ALSO TRUE FACT -- flat days.  A trend book that is out of
  the market returns EXACTLY zero, and the strict reading cannot count a
  zero as profitable, so the book's own share of positive days understates
  its in-market win rate.  Counted:

  book  days  flat days  % flat  D>0 @lam1  D>=0 @lam1  Y>0 @lam1  Y>=0 @lam1

  R1L   8285  2111       25.5%   40.6%      66.1%       65.6%      68.8%

  The loose reading does NOT move the monthly/quarterly/yearly endpoints:
  a cash sliver of 0.8% already makes any flat month positive, so the two
  readings agree on every bucket from M outward.  It does not rescue the
  daily endpoint either, because 2 days in 21 years had a strictly
  NEGATIVE cash accrual (section 2).

  HOW THIN THE YEAR ENDPOINT IS.  The binding year for the primary book is
  2015, the year with the smallest return at the endpoint:
    lambda=0.008 -- 2015 returns 0.0038%; % of years positive 100%
    lambda=0.009 -- 2015 -0.0017%
    The endpoint is decided by ONE year, and the whole difference
    between 'positive in every year' and 'not positive in every year'
    is 0.0056 percentage points of that one year's return.
