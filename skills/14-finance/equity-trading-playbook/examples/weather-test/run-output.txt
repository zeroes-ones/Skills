
===== SECTION: 0 · METHOD AND PROVENANCE =====
  PRICE-ONLY DATA. No dividends, no distributions. Every series is a
  price series, not a total-return series. Any always-long sleeve is
  therefore understated relative to a total-return version, and the
  always-long comparison sleeve G most of all.

  Sharpe convention: daily simple returns, full sample, Rf = 0, x sqrt(252).
  CAGR: compounded end/start, annualised over calendar time (365.25 d/yr).
  Max drawdown (headline): COMPOUNDED peak-to-trough of the equity curve,
    as a negative fraction -- the sibling regime study's definition, so the
    two studies' drawdown columns compare directly.
  Max drawdown (additive): the sibling engine's max_drawdown() on the daily
    return series -- peak-to-trough of the cumulated returns, in return units.
    Quoted for the book only, and only to show the two measures differ.
  Execution: signal on close of day i, position held through day i+1.
  Warm-up: a sleeve is FLAT until its moving average exists.
  Rebalance: monthly, to the pre-registered weights; weights drift in between.
  Costs: OFF here; on in section 11.

  Engine imported, not re-implemented: C:\Users\sande\Documents\Projects\Skills\skills\14-finance\timeframe-performance-statistics\scripts\timeframe_stats.py
    used: bucket_key(), aggregate_bucket(), sample_sd(), max_drawdown(),

===== SECTION: 6 · ENGINE CROSS-CHECK ON THE YEARLY SERIES (aggregate_bucket, bucket='Y') =====
  The sibling engine is fed one pseudo-trade per book day per sleeve:
  close_date = the day, pnl = that day's sleeve return. Its `net_pnl` is
  therefore the ADDITIVE sum of daily returns in the year, not the
  compounded return of section 4 -- the two agree closely in small years
  and diverge in large ones. Its t_stat tests whether the mean yearly P&L
  is distinguishable from zero across the years; its lag1/n_eff say how
  much independent information the yearly series actually carries.

  sleeve  n_years  mean_yr_pnl  sd_yr_pnl  t_stat  lag1_autocorr  n_eff  beyond +/-2
  ------  -------  -----------  ---------  ------  -------------  -----  -----------
  A       22       0.06651      0.11478    2.718   -0.227         35.0   yes
  B       22       -0.00032     0.10411    -0.014  -0.110         27.4   no
  C       22       0.08716      0.16028    2.550   0.234          13.6   yes
  D       22       0.03813      0.15913    1.124   -0.136         28.9   no
  E       22       -0.30605     0.58062    -2.472  -0.339         44.5   yes
  G       22       0.10115      0.15904    2.983   -0.190         32.3   yes

  t_stat beyond the engine's own +/-2.0 noise band means the sleeve's
  yearly mean is large relative to its year-to-year scatter. n_eff below
  n_years means the yearly series is positively autocorrelated and the
  evidence is thinner than the year count suggests.

===== SECTION: 12 · WHAT WAS NOT DONE, AND WHAT REMAINS UNTESTED =====
  Stated as limitations, not as caveats to be skipped.

  1. SLEEVE E IS NOT TRADABLE. The VIX index cannot be bought. Its return
     here is the index level change while the rule is long; a real long-vol
     position is a VIX futures or futures-ETN position, which bleeds on roll
     in contango. No roll cost, no ETN fee, no borrow is modelled. Every E
     number is an UPPER BOUND on the signal and is not an achievable return.
     Drop E and the book is portfolio F, whose row is above.
  2. PRICE-ONLY. No dividends or distributions anywhere. TLT's price
     multiple over 2002-2026 is 0.9561 in the sibling study, i.e. TLT's
     price is BELOW its start -- so sleeve B's always-long component, and
     the bond leg generally, is understated versus a total-return version.
     SPY/GLD/EEM pay distributions too. Buy & hold G is understated most.
  3. LONG ONLY, NO LEVERAGE, NO SHORTS. The book cannot short a downtrend;
     it can only stand aside. That is a design choice, not a finding.
  4. ONE PARAMETER SET. SMA(200) for A-D and SMA(50) for E were fixed
     before the run. This study does NOT establish that 200 beats 150, nor
     that 50 beats 20, and does not rule either out. Parameter sensitivity
     is untested.
  5. FIVE SLEEVES, ALL TREND-SHAPED except E. A-D are the same rule on four
     different instruments. Section 5 measures how much that actually
     diversifies; it is the honest answer to 'is this four bets or one'.
  6. ONE WINDOW. The book needs all five series, so it starts when gold's
     SMA(200) exists. It therefore does not cover 1993-2004 at all. Sleeve A
     and G are also shown on full history for that reason. The window is
     dominated by the post-2009 bull market, which flatters every long-only
     sleeve and the always-long comparison most.
  7. NO REGIME TEST here. The sibling regime study did that; this study only
     reports years and drawdowns.
  8. THE LAST BAR WAS STILL FORMING at pull time and may revise.
