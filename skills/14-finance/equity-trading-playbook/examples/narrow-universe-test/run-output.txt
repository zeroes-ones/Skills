-- 0.4 calendar construction, and the one series excluded from it
  The book calendar is the intersection of the PRICE instruments' own
  calendars, from the last date on which every sleeve's SMA(200) rule is
  computable.  The cash series is excluded from the intersection on
  purpose -- see below -- and its last known rate is carried forward.
  US03M is why the exclusion matters:
      US03M has NO observation on 483 of W14's 3422 days (14.1%).
      Examples: 2013-03-15, 2013-03-22, 2013-03-28, 2013-04-05, 2013-04-12
      Those are real US trading sessions where the bill yield index
      simply has no print.  Intersecting on it would have deleted them
      from every book in this study.  cash_returns() carries the rate
      forward across the gap instead, which is the conservative choice
      (the rate is stale, never invented).
  Price-instrument gaps: JNJ and XOM each lack a bar on 2024-11-07, so
  the intersection simply does not contain that session -- it is dropped
  from the calendar rather than carried.  After intersection, 0 day(s)
  remain on which a member of a book has no bar.  The sleeve convention
  on such a day is to carry its position and count the price move at the
  next available close.  One day in 3400 moves no statistic here.
