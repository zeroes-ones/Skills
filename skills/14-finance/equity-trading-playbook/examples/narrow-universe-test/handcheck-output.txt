  THE EXACT IDENTITY, stated so it can be checked by eye:
      A portfolio held with DRIFTING weights from the start of a period
      earns the START-WEIGHTED AVERAGE of its legs' period returns --
      no rebalancing inside the period means each leg compounds alone.
      So   book month return  ==  sum_i w_i0 * (leg i month return).
      trend : -0.0128971161  vs  -0.0128971161   (gap -5.2e-16)
      plain : -0.0918379716  vs  -0.0918379716   (gap 2.1e-16)

  WHAT WOULD HAVE BEEN WRONG: compounding the daily returns with the
  weights RE-SET to 1/14 every day instead of left to drift gives
  -0.0124796377 for the trend book, out by 4.2e-04 against the book.
  That is the difference between a DAILY-rebalanced portfolio and the
  MONTHLY-rebalanced one this study actually simulates, and it is the
  single easiest way to get this arithmetic wrong.

  CALENDAR SENSITIVITY, reported rather than hidden:
  the same year on the raw SPY+QQQ intersection calendar gives
