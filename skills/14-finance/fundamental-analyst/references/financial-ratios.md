# Financial Ratios — Complete Catalog

## Profitability Ratios
- Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue (trend > stability)
- Operating Margin = Operating Income / Revenue
- Net Margin = Net Income / Revenue (check for one-time items)
- ROE = Net Income / Shareholders' Equity (DuPont decomposition required)
- ROA = Net Income / Total Assets
- ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital (must exceed WACC)

## Valuation Ratios
- P/E (Trailing) = Price / EPS (last 4 quarters, GAAP)
- P/E (Forward) = Price / Consensus EPS Estimate (next 4 quarters)
- P/E (Adjusted) = Price / EPS (excluding one-time items)
- PEG = P/E / EPS Growth Rate (3-5 year CAGR)
- EV/EBITDA = Enterprise Value / EBITDA
- P/B = Price / Book Value per Share
- P/S = Price / Sales per Share (for unprofitable growth companies)
- P/FCF = Price / Free Cash Flow per Share
- FCF Yield = FCF / Market Cap (>5% attractive, >8% very attractive)

## Financial Health Ratios
- Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities (>1.5 healthy)
- Quick Ratio = (CA - Inventory) / CL (>1.0 healthy)
- Debt/Equity = Total Debt / Total Equity (<2.0 ex-financials)
- Debt/EBITDA = Total Debt / EBITDA (<3.0 healthy, >5.0 risky)
- Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense (>3.0 minimum)
- FCF/Debt = FCF / Total Debt (ability to pay down debt)

## Efficiency Ratios
- Asset Turnover = Revenue / Total Assets
- Inventory Turnover = COGS / Average Inventory
- Receivables Turnover = Revenue / Average Receivables
- Days Sales Outstanding = 365 / Receivables Turnover (rising = red flag)

## Growth Metrics
- Revenue Growth (YoY, 3Y CAGR, 5Y CAGR) — accelerating = bullish
- EPS Growth — decompose into: revenue growth + margin expansion + buybacks + one-time
- FCF Growth — more important than EPS growth (harder to manipulate)
- Dividend Growth (3Y, 5Y CAGR)

## Dividend Ratios
- Dividend Yield = Annual Dividend / Price
- Payout Ratio = Dividends / Net Income (<60% sustainable)
- FCF Payout Ratio = Dividends / FCF (if >100%, dividends debt-financed)
- Dividend Coverage = EPS / Dividend per Share (>2.0 safe)
