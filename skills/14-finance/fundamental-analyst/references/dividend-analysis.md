# Dividend Analysis — Sustainability and Quality

## Dividend Safety Check
1. Payout Ratio (EPS-based) < 60%: SAFE
   - 60-80%: MONITOR — limited buffer for earnings decline
   - >80%: RISK — any earnings decline forces a cut
2. FCF Payout Ratio (FCF-based) < 70%: SAFE
   - >100%: DANGER — dividends financed by debt/share issuance
3. Interest Coverage > 3.0: debt-servicing capacity before dividends
4. Debt/EBITDA < 3.0: leverage manageable

## Dividend Growth Quality
- 5+ years of consecutive dividend growth: positive signal
- Growth rate exceeding EPS growth: unsustainable — eating into retention
- Dividend cut in past 5 years: permanent scar — management prioritizes buybacks over dividends
- Special dividends: not part of recurring yield — do not annualize

## Sector Expectations
- Utilities: 3-5% yield, 2-4% growth — steady compounders
- Consumer Staples: 2-3% yield, 5-8% growth — reliable
- REITs: 4-6% yield, 2-4% growth — 90% payout required by law
- Tech: 0-1.5% yield, 8-15% growth — capital return via buybacks
- Energy MLPs: 6-10% yield, 0-2% growth — distribution-based, high risk

## Dividend Cut Warning Signals
- Payout ratio rising 20%+ in one year (earnings declining faster than dividend)
- FCF turning negative while dividend maintained
- Debt increasing while dividend maintained
- CEO/CFO departure (new management often cuts to "reset" expectations)
- Industry peers cutting dividends (sector-wide stress)
