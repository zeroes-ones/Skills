# Earnings Quality — Detecting Manipulation

## Accruals Analysis
Cash Flow from Operations - Net Income = Total Accruals
Persistent negative accruals (OCF < NI) over 3+ years = aggressive accounting.
Accruals Ratio = (NI - OCF) / Total Assets. >5% = investigate.

## Revenue Recognition Red Flags
- Receivables growing >20% faster than revenue → channel stuffing
- Days Sales Outstanding increasing 3+ quarters → extending payment terms to boost sales
- Revenue spike in Q4 significantly higher than Q1-Q3 → year-end push
- Bill-and-hold arrangements → revenue recognized before delivery
- Related-party revenue >5% of total → non-arm's-length transactions

## Expense Manipulation Red Flags
- Capitalizing operating expenses (R&D, marketing as "software development")
- Extending depreciation lives (reducing depreciation expense artificially)
- Understating warranty/return reserves
- Restructuring charges EVERY year (one-time should be one-time)
- Cookie jar reserves: over-reserving in good years, releasing in bad years

## Cash Flow Red Flags
- FCF consistently below Net Income (gap >20% of NI) → earnings not converting to cash
- Capex < Depreciation for 3+ years → under-investing, consuming asset base
- Working capital ballooning (inventory + receivables growing faster than revenue)
- Stock-based compensation >10% of revenue → real dilution hidden from GAAP EPS
- Dividends > FCF → financing payouts with debt

## Auditor Red Flags
- Auditor change within past 12 months (especially to smaller firm)
- Going concern opinion
- Material weakness in internal controls
- Late filings (10-K, 10-Q extensions)
- Restatements of prior financials
