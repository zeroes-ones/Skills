# ETF Fundamentals — Beyond Past Performance

## Primary Screening Criteria (in priority order)
1. Expense Ratio: lower = more return retained by investor
2. Tracking Error (1Y): how closely ETF tracks its index
3. AUM: >$100M for viability, >$500M for institutional quality
4. Holdings: full replication > sampling (lower tracking error)
5. Premium/Discount: persistent premium = poor liquidity or creation/redemption friction

## Expense Ratio Impact
$10,000 invested for 30 years at 7% annual return:
- 0.03% ER (VOO): $76,123 (cost: $1,233)
- 0.50% ER (average active ETF): $69,452 (cost: $9,880)
- 0.75% ER (thematic): $63,832 (cost: $15,500)
Difference 0.03% vs 0.75% = $14,267 lost to fees.

## Thematic ETF Risks
- 67% of thematic ETFs underperform broad market over 5 years
- Launched after theme 100%+ rally: underperform by 6-8%/year
- Closure risk: 3x index ETF closure rate (AUM <$50M = high closure risk)
- Holdings overlap >60% with broad market: buy the cheaper index fund

## Leveraged/Inverse ETF Risks
- Daily reset: compounding ≠ leverage × underlying return for >1 day
- Volatility decay: sideways markets erode value
- 3x bull + 3x bear both lose money in volatile flat markets
- NOT suitable for buy-and-hold

## Bond ETF Considerations
- Yield to Maturity > ER + 0.50% for net positive real return
- Duration matching: duration ≈ years until you need the money
- Credit quality distribution: % below investment grade
- Average maturity: longer maturity = higher rate sensitivity
