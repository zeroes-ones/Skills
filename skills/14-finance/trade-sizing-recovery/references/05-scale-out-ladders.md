# Scale-Out Ladders — Cheat Card

## Rungs by position size

| Position size | Rung 1 | Rung 2 | Runner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 lot | 100% at first rung (≥1.8× cost) — or at the level that kills the thesis, whichever comes first | — | — |
| 2 lots | 1 lot at ~1.8× | 1 lot at ~2.5× (or trail after rung 1) | — |
| 3 lots | 1 lot at 1.8× | 1 lot at 2.5× | 1 lot, invalidation trailed to entry |

**Free-trade rule:** once rung sales total ≥ the original debit, the remaining runner is a lottery ticket with zero rent owed. Let it ride to the level that kills the thesis, not to a feeling.

## Worked example (2 lots, debit $1.50/contract = $300 total)

| Action | Price | Proceeds | Running P&L |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy 2 lots | 1.50 | −$300 | −$300 |
| Sell 1 @ 1.8× | 2.70 | +$270 | −$30 (position now free) |
| Sell 1 @ 2.5× | 3.75 | +$375 | +$345 booked |
| If instead both @ 1.8× | 2.70 | +$540 | +$240 (less, but simpler) |

The ladder trades a little EV for consistency — worth it if inconsistency is what breaks the plan; skip rung 2 only when conviction is ≥8/10 AND the invalidation is tight.

## Mechanics that matter

- **All rungs are GTC sell-to-close orders, placed at entry.** No "I'll decide later".
- **Respect venue sessions** — RTH-only orders will not fill pre/post, however good the print looks.
- **Thin books:** rungs at round numbers (1.80 / 2.00 / 2.50) fill more often than odd values; check OI at the strike first.
- **After a rung sells, the invalidation tightens** — a half-booked winner gets its stop pulled up, not left at the original level.
- **Do not move TP levels away from price.** If the plan says 2.5× and price is 2.2× and stalling, that is a hold-or-cut decision at the level, not a re-pricing.
