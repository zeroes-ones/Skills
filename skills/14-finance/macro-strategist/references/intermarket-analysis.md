# Intermarket Analysis

## Key Intermarket Ratios
| Ratio | Formula | Signal | Interpretation |
|-------|---------|--------|----------------|
| Equity/Bond | SPY / TLT | Risk appetite | Rising = risk-on (equities outperforming bonds) |
| Copper/Gold | Copper futures / Gold futures | Growth optimism | Rising = industrial demand > safe haven |
| HYG/TLT | High-yield bonds / Long Treasuries | Credit risk appetite | Rising = credit risk-taking |
| Cyclicals/Defensives | XLY / XLP | Equity sector rotation | Rising = growth optimism |
| MOVE/VIX | Bond vol / Equity vol | Rate vol vs equity vol | Rising = bond vol dominating, rate-driven regime |
