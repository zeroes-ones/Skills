# Exchange Due Diligence

## Proof of Reserves Checklist
- [ ] Last attestation date (should be <6 months old)
- [ ] Auditor firm (Mazars, Armanino preferred; self-attestation = high risk)
- [ ] Reserves > Liabilities ratio (>1.0x minimum; >1.05x preferred)
- [ ] On-chain address verification (can reserves be traced on-chain?)
- [ ] Fiat reserves: bank attestation letter (harder to verify than crypto)

## Withdrawal Risk Signals
- **Red flags**: Social media reports of withdrawal delays, reduced daily limits, "wallet maintenance"
- **Freeze history**: Has the exchange ever frozen withdrawals? When? For how long?
- **Withdrawal fees**: Compare across exchanges; high fees = friction during stress
- **Minimum withdrawal**: Small holders may be trapped below minimum thresholds
