## Tax-Loss Harvesting
- Sell losers to offset capital gains
- **Wash sale rule**: Cannot repurchase "substantially identical" security within 30 days before or after sale
- Offset up to $3,000/year of ordinary income
- Unlimited capital gain offset
- Carry forward unused losses indefinitely

## Key Optimization Strategies
1. **Roth conversion ladders**: Convert during low-income years (between retirement and RMDs)
2. **Asset location**: Bonds in tax-deferred, stocks in taxable/Roth
3. **Charitable giving**: Donate appreciated stock (avoid LTCG + get deduction)
4. **Tax-gain harvesting**: Realize gains in 0% LTCG bracket years
5. **Qualified dividends**: Hold ≥60 days for lower tax rate

## State Tax Considerations
- 9 states with no income tax: AK, FL, NV, NH, SD, TN, TX, WA, WY
- Relocating in retirement can save 5-13% state tax on withdrawals
- Source-based taxation for remote workers: where you work, not where employer is
